<p>
I lead ATI Labs, a corporate venture inside Incheon International Airport Corporation, building AI that runs airport operations on its own.
</p>

<p>
Airports still make thousands of operational decisions by hand every day — carousels, aircraft stands, check-in counters, safety plans, construction phasing, planning and office work — each with its own rules and legacy systems. We automate and optimize them one task at a time, validate each against the real operation, connect them behind one interface, and work toward coordinating them so the whole airport runs at its best. Airports first; ports, rail and logistics next.
</p>

<p>
Background: I joined the airport in 2015 as an electrical engineer and spent eight years on airfield power and SMGCS. While working full time, I completed a master's in computer science at Georgia Tech in 2020 and kept doing machine learning research on my own, including a Best Paper at the ACL 2021 RepL4NLP workshop. Since 2024 I have worked in ML full time, building the airport's passenger-flow simulation and prediction system, which helped keep departure processing within international standards through record-breaking 2025 traffic.
</p>
